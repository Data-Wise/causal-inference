Introduction

This document outlines the suggested weekly assignments for STAT 440/540: Regression Analysis. The problems are from the required textbook, Applied Linear Regression Models, 4th ed. by Kutner, Nachtsheim, and Neter (2004).

The assignments are structured to provide a solid foundation for all students, with additional theoretical and applied problems for graduate students enrolled in STAT 540 to ensure greater depth.

Important

This list is a guide. The official and final list of problems, along with due dates and submission guidelines, will be posted on Canvas. Please refer to Canvas for all definitive assignment information.

Assignment Policies

  • Submission: All assignments must be submitted via Canvas.
  • Grade Weight: Assignments account for 30% of the final grade.
  • Late Submissions: A 10% penalty per day is applied for a maximum of three days. Assignments will not be accepted after this period.

Weekly Problem Sets

Week 1: Introduction to Regression

Topics: Review of probability/stats; history and applications of regression. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 1.1, 1.2, App. A, B

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 1.3, 1.5, 1.8, 1.15
    • Projects: 1.19, 1.21
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: A.3, B.4 (from Appendix)

Week 2: Simple Linear Regression

Topics: Model specification; parameter estimation (Least Squares, MLE). Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 1.5–1.8

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 1.6, 1.7, 1.10, 1.27
    • Projects: 1.20, 1.24
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 1.30, 1.35 (Prove that the sum of residuals is zero and that the sample mean of Y is on the regression line).

Week 3: Inference in Regression

Topics: Hypothesis testing; confidence and prediction intervals; ANOVA. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 2.1–2.7

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 2.1, 2.3, 2.5, 2.13, 2.15
    • Projects: 2.29, 2.34, 2.41
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 2.20, 2.25 (Derive the expected value of MSR and MSE).

Week 4: Diagnostics & Remedial Measures

Topics: Residual analysis; outlier detection; tests for model assumptions. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 3

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 3.1, 3.4, 3.8, 3.10
    • Projects: 3.14, 3.17
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 3.13 (Show the equivalence of two forms of the F-test for lack of fit).

Week 5: Midterm Exam 1

There is no assignment this week. Focus on reviewing for the midterm exam.

Week 6: Simultaneous Inference

Topics: Joint estimation; Bonferroni & Working-Hotelling procedures. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 4

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 4.2, 4.5, 4.9, 4.11
    • Projects: 4.18, 4.20
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 4.15 (Derive the Working-Hotelling confidence band).

Week 7: Matrix Approach to SLR

Topics: Regression model in matrix terms; estimation and inference. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 5

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 5.1, 5.4, 5.9, 5.12, 5.20
    • Projects: 5.25, 5.28
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 5.15, 5.23 (Work with the hat matrix H and prove properties of residuals).

Week 8: Multiple Linear Regression I

Topics: Model specification with multiple predictors; parameter interpretation. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 6

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 6.1, 6.3, 6.5, 6.9, 6.15
    • Projects: 6.19, 6.23
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 6.17 (Show that the coefficient of multiple determination \(R^2\) cannot decrease as predictors are added).

Week 9: Multiple Linear Regression II

Topics: Extra sums of squares; tests for coefficients; standardized models. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 7

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 7.2, 7.6, 7.8, 7.12, 7.17
    • Projects: 7.25, 7.30
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 7.20, 7.22 (Theory behind the general linear F-test).

Week 10: Midterm Exam 2

There is no assignment this week. Focus on reviewing for the midterm exam.

Week 11 & 12: Interaction, Polynomial, & Qualitative Models

Topics: Fitting polynomial models; interaction terms; indicator variables. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 8

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 8.2, 8.5, 8.8, 8.10, 8.15
    • Projects: 8.18, 8.21, 8.29
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 8.17, 8.26 (Derive the meaning of coefficients in a model with interaction between quantitative and qualitative variables).

Week 13: Model Selection and Validation

Topics: Criteria for model selection (Cp, AIC, PRESS); stepwise procedures. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 9

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 9.3, 9.6, 9.10, 9.12
    • Projects: 9.15, 9.19
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 9.14 (Relate \(R^2\) to the PRESS statistic).

Week 14 & 15: Advanced Diagnostics & Remedial Measures

Topics: Influential cases (Cook’s D); multicollinearity (VIFs); Ridge Regression. Reading: Kutner et al., Ch. 10 & 11

  • Core Assignment (STAT 440/540):
    • Problems: 10.2, 10.8, 10.12, 11.1, 11.7, 11.19
    • Projects: 10.15, 11.25
  • Graduate Assignment (STAT 540):
    • Exercises: 10.14 (Derive Cook’s distance), 11.16 (Show the effect of multicollinearity on variance of coefficients).

Week 16: Final Review & Course Project

There is no weekly problem set.

Course Project Due

The final course project is due this week. Graduate students (STAT 540) are expected to incorporate more advanced modeling techniques, perform a more rigorous model validation, and include a literature review component in their final report.
