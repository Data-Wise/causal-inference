Rubric Breakdown (100 points)

Category Weight Criteria
Question & Relevance 10 pts Is the question causal? Clearly motivated?
DAG & Identification 20 pts DAG correct & justified, assumptions transparent, adjustment sets valid
Methodology 20 pts Appropriate causal method chosen, correctly implemented, diagnostics included
Sensitivity & Robustness 15 pts At least one sensitivity analysis, well-explained
Results & Interpretation 15 pts Estimates clearly reported, assumptions tied to conclusions, limitations addressed
Writing & Presentation 20 pts Paper clarity, organization, reproducibility (R code provided), presentation quality
